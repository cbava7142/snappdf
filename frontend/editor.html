About SnapPDF

Hi, I'm Muhammad Umer, an undergraduate Computer Science student based in Rahim Yar Khan, Pakistan.

I built SnapPDF with a simple goal: to provide a fast, beautiful, and 100% free toolkit for everyday PDF tasks. There are no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and most importantly, no privacy risks—your files are processed securely in memory and deleted instantly after downloading.

Built With

Frontend: HTML, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JS
Backend: Python, Django REST Framework
Authentication: Clerk
